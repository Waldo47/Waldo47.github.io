A Lot Of Bullets

A Lot Of Bullets

In development

A 2D roguelite shooter where the order of your bullet effects changes the bullet. Effects run as a pipeline: each one acts on the output of the one above it, and the player can reorder them freely. Your own bullets don't hurt you, but the explosions, puddles and mines they spawn do, so a wild combo can end your run. A two-person project with Maximiliano Caneda, with outsourced art.

What I did: Set up the architecture (service locator, object pooling) and built the gameplay systems: the effect pipeline, where effects listed after a split travel to the child bullets, elemental damage with status effects, enemies and wave scaling, bosses that assemble random attack patterns, and the shop with rarity rolls and rerolls. Built a stress-test scene to measure the game under normal, heavy and extreme load, and used the results to guide optimization. Currently tuning the game balance.
